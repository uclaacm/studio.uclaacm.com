CONTENT TRIM LOG
Shrinking the six oversized homepage snap blocks so each fits one screen.
Every change is listed with its exact before -> after so it can be reverted
individually. Nothing else in the repo was touched by this pass.
================================================================================

WHY
---
Six blocks were taller than the scrollport, so scrolling past them took more
than one gesture. Measured at a 632px viewport, where each block gets 515px:

    block                 height   padding-btm   CONTENT   over by
    SRSHome                 658          0         658       144
    Resources               766        170         596       251
    TryGameJams             739        170         569       224
    StudioJams              773        170         603       259
    Community (socials)     588        170         417        73
    E1Home                  754        170         583       239

Two earlier attempts (scroll-snap `proximity`, and reclaiming ~138px of the
height budget) were reverted - neither was enough, because even at ZERO padding
five of the six still had more content than 515px. The content itself had to
come down. That is what this pass does.

RESULT
------
Verified in the browser: all 17 snap blocks on the homepage now fit, none
overflow. Measurement harness read real layout from an animation frame
(layout reads return 0 in a backgrounded tab, so this was the only reliable
way to measure here).

    before: 6 of 17 blocks overflowed
    after:  0 of 17 overflow

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Reported: Speaker Series, Studio Jam, SRS and Zero to Hero did not feel
vertically centred when snapped to.

Measured cause: EVERY event body had `gapTop: 0`. Content was pinned to the top
of the block and all the slack piled up underneath it. Several files tried to

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN ISSUE FOUND BUT NOT FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LearnByDoing (the first Workshops block) CLIPS its content on short screens.
It uses `height:` (fixed) plus `overflow: "clip"`, so it can never overflow
dimensionally - it silently truncates instead. That is why it never appeared in
the "takes two scrolls" list. At a 632px viewport it sits exactly at its limit
(content 515 = height 515), so any shorter viewport starts cutting content off.

Losing content is arguably worse than an extra scroll. Fixing it means choosing:
  - `height` -> `minHeight` stops the truncation but reintroduces the scroll
    problem on short screens, or
  - trim its content the way the other five blocks were trimmed.
